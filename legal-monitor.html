Product

AI Legal Monitor

A daily legal-change briefing and assistant that helps Croatian accountants understand what changed—without digging through legislation.

The problem

Legal changes are easy to miss.

Croatia’s official gazette publishes new laws and amendments daily. Keeping track manually is time-consuming and makes it easy to overlook changes that matter.

What I built

A focused daily monitor.

The product scans new publications, identifies relevant changes, and lets accountants ask direct questions grounded in legal text and official interpretations.

How it works

Daily scanClassify changesSend relevant alertsAnswer practical questions

The result

Less hunting, more clarity.

A practical starting point for turning a large, changing body of regulation into useful, timely information.
