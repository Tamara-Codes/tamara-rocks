Product

Welcome Book

A digital guest guide for holiday rentals. One QR code gives guests the essentials in their own language—before they need to ask.

Visit Welcome Book →

The idea

Guests ask the same questions.

Wi-Fi, check-out, beaches, restaurants, house rules, and ferry times. Hosts should not need to send the same answers again and again.

The product

Scan once. Find what you need.

A QR code opens a complete, mobile-first guide with apartment information, useful local details, and direct links to calls, WhatsApp, and Maps.

Proof

3 paying customers

Welcome Book is a product I built and sold to accommodation businesses.

What I built

  • QR-led guest experience
  • Six-language guide
  • Reusable setup for new apartments
  • Mobile-first information design
