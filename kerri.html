Client project

Studio KERRI Design

A visual catalogue website that brings a long-established Matulji showroom’s tile, bathroom, and interior collection online.

Visit Studio KERRI Design ↗

The problem

A rich showroom needed a clearer online home.

Studio KERRI represents dozens of brands across tiles, sanitaryware, furniture, baths, and shower cabins. Visitors needed a more direct way to explore that range before they came to the showroom.

What I built

A visual catalogue, not a brochure.

I built a polished website that leads with the products, makes categories easy to explore, and gives every represented brand room to feel considered.

How it works

Choose a product categoryExplore the collectionDiscover brandsGet in touch with the showroom

My role

Structure, visual direction, and build.

I shaped the site’s information architecture, designed the browsing experience, and developed the responsive Next.js website.

What it changes

A stronger first impression.

  • A clear path through a large product range
  • More space for premium imagery and brands
  • A showroom experience that starts before the visit
