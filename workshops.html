A few things you might be wondering

Do I need to attend the free workshop first?

No. AI Demystified is a useful introduction if you’re new to AI, but you can request a place in the paid workshop directly if lead generation and outreach are what you want to explore.

Do I need Claude, ChatGPT or Apify already set up?

You can follow the examples on my screen without setting up accounts. If you’d like to try the workflow during the session, have your accounts ready and bring your questions. I’ll make time to help. Tool subscriptions and usage credits aren’t included in the €49 ticket.

What happens after I reserve a place?

Your place is held in your chosen session, and you’ll receive a reservation email. I’ll send your payment link once checkout is ready. You’ll have 48 hours from that email to pay the €49 and confirm your booking. If all eight places are reserved, you can join the waiting list.
