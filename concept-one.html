Client project

Concept One

A website and offer system that turns a broad construction offering into a clearer, more confident sales story.

The problem

One business, many services.

Concept One needed a clearer way to present a wide range of building products and services, then guide prospective customers toward the right conversation.

What I built

A website plus sales toolkit.

I created a focused marketing site with clear service paths, along with a custom offer presentation system to support sales conversations beyond the website.

The website

A stronger first impression.

Each product category gets room to explain its value while the wider brand stays coherent and easy to navigate.

My role

Strategy, design, and build.

I shaped the structure and visual direction, built the website, and designed the offer materials that make the service easier to explain and sell.

What it changes

Clarity at every step.

  • Clearer service categories
  • A polished first impression
  • A consistent offer for sales conversations
